Postdoctoral Research Associate

Department of Astronomy, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

I work with Yue Shen at UIUC on the coevolution of supermassive black holes and their host galaxies. My research combines JWST, HST, and ground-based imaging and spectroscopy to study galaxy formation and evolution, AGN outflows and feedback, the dusty torus around accreting black holes, and high-redshift transients and supernovae.

Research

My work combines multi-wavelength imaging and spectroscopy — much of it from JWST — to understand how active galactic nuclei and their host galaxies grow and evolve together across cosmic time.

01

SMBH – galaxy coevolution

How black holes and their host galaxies grow together, from local ellipticals to quasars at cosmic noon.

02

Galaxy formation & evolution

The assembly and evolution of galaxies from the early universe to today, using deep imaging and spectroscopy.

03

High-z transients & supernovae

Time-domain discoveries of distant supernovae and transients, including with the NEXUS JWST survey.

04

AGN outflows & feedback

How energy and gas ejected by active galactic nuclei shape star formation in their host galaxies.

05

AGN dusty torus

The structure, geometry, and infrared signatures of the obscuring torus around accreting supermassive black holes.
